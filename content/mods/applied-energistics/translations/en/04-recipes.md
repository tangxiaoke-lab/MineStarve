## Wireless Storage Terminal: normal difficulty

The Wireless Storage Terminal costs Restrained Static ×1, Scrap ×4 and Pure Brilliance ×4. It requires Alchemy Engine technology and wireless terminals enabled in the world.

> Easy and hard difficulty change the costs of expansion cards, Blank Patterns and wireless terminals. The normal-difficulty quantities above do not apply to those other difficulties.
