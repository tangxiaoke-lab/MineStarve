# Equipment and Upgrades

Gold, Copper, Iron, Diamond and Netherite swords, axes, pickaxes, helmets and chestplates add a material progression and inlays.

## Crafting progression

Copper equipment uses the matching base game item. Iron equipment requires the corresponding Copper item, Diamond equipment requires Iron, and Netherite equipment requires Diamond. Gold equipment is crafted separately.

| Equipment | Material | Other requirements | Technology |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Axe or pickaxe | Ingots or Diamonds ×3 | Twigs ×4; Gold uses ×6, Netherite ×12; upgrades also use the previous item | Depends on material |
| Sword | Ingots or Diamonds ×6 | Twigs ×4; upgrades also use the previous sword | Depends on material |
| Helmet | Ingots or Diamonds ×5 | Previous helmet when upgrading | Depends on material |
| Chestplate | Ingots or Diamonds ×8 | Previous chestplate when upgrading | Depends on material |

Copper and Gold need no technology; Iron requires a Science Machine, Diamond needs tier 3 magic technology, and Netherite requires an Ancient station. Copper consumes the corresponding base equipment; later tiers consume the previous tier.

## Traits and inlays

Each Gold equipment piece grants an 8% movement-speed bonus and increases damage against shadow creatures. Holding a wet Copper tool increases incoming electric damage. Higher-tier pickaxes can mine harder ores.

| Target | Inlay material | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diamond Helmet or Chestplate | Matching Dreadstone helmet or armour | Gains the relevant Dreadstone regeneration and set traits |
| Diamond Sword | Brightshade Sword or Voidcloth Scythe | Gains the corresponding planar damage and faction bonus |
| Netherite sword, axe, pickaxe, helmet or chestplate | Celestial Jewel | Restores full durability and prevents further durability loss |

Hold the material and use the Inlay action on compatible equipment. The material is consumed, and the same kind of inlay cannot be installed twice.
