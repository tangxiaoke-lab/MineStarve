# Minerals and Machines

Mine copper, iron and Nether Quartz, process minerals in the Oven, and power Mining Machines.

## Ore and tool tiers

| Ore | Area | Minimum pickaxe tier | Drop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Copper Ore | Surface rocky areas | Tier 1; a standard pickaxe works | Raw Copper |
| Iron Ore | Surface badlands | Tier 2; a Copper Pickaxe works | Raw Iron |
| Nether Quartz Ore | Cave fumarole areas | Tier 3; an Iron Pickaxe works | Nether Quartz |

Mining an ore leaves a mine hole. The hole can be worked further: after every 20 actions, it has a 20% base chance to yield ore, increased by 10 percentage points for each additional pickaxe tier. A hole without a Mining Machine regenerates into ore after about 24 minutes.

## Oven and metals

The Smelting Oven costs Dreadstone ×4, Cut Stone ×6 and Boards ×3 at an Alchemy Engine. Put material and fuel into its slots, then collect the output. One Raw Gold yields 2 Gold Nuggets. Coal, Raw Copper, Raw Iron and Gold Nuggets can also be heated for subsequent forging. One Coal supports four batches; Charcoal supports two Raw Gold batches, but cannot fuel the heat-processing recipes.

Place a stack of at least 3 heated Raw Copper, Raw Iron or Gold Nuggets on the ground and repeatedly hammer it. The process consumes 3 pieces to make 1 matching ingot. Cooling, rain and water affect the heat process; Gold Nuggets and Raw Gold have different uses.

Inlay 3 Celestial Jewels to upgrade the Smelting Oven into a Celestial Furnace. It can then attempt to turn Dreadstone and Voidcloth into a Netherite Ingot: each Dreadstone consumes 3 Voidcloth, with a 2% success chance.

## Automated mining and power

The Modern Mining Machine Kit costs Iron Ingots ×4, Copper Ingots ×2, Gears ×2 and Boards ×4 at an Alchemy Engine. Install it on an already mined hole. It produces the mineral matching that hole, roughly once every 60 seconds. It accepts an external power connection or Nightmare Fuel or Pure Horror in its fuel slot. Its panel shows operating state and stored energy.

The Nightmare Fuel Generator Mk I costs Iron Ingots ×4, Copper Ingots ×2, Gears ×2 and Nightmare Fuel ×2 at an Alchemy Engine. It powers Mining Machines and compatible engineering devices. Nightmare Fuel and Pure Horror can both fuel it.

> Working a mine hole manually and automating it are separate ways to obtain ore. Install the machine on the hole, not on an intact ore deposit.
