# The Deep Dark

Sail to the Sculk island, face three bosses in Charlie's arena, and decide whether to begin the Pixel Invasion.

## Finding the island and starting the challenge

The Deep Dark is a separate island at sea. It contains Sculk Ground, Sculk Veins and Charlie's arena. New worlds generate the island; eligible existing worlds also try to add it. Approaching Charlie starts the performance and battle sequence.

During the challenge, the arena restricts entry, exit and teleportation. If the party falls, it has a 60-second revival window; the challenge fails if nobody recovers in time. The next boss arrives about 10 seconds after the previous one is defeated.

## Boss order

| Order | Boss | Main threats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Void-Devouring Worm King | Burrowing, swallowing, spines and Life Siphon, which repeatedly drains nearby targets. |
| 2 | Rift Bat King | Summoned bats and clones, poison, healing lockout and fear. |
| 3 | Meteor Lobster King | Rock Lobster Guards, charges, quakes, meteor rain and Resonant Meteor Boulders. |

## Void-Devouring Worm King: break the siphon

| Mechanic | Counter |
| --- | --- |
| The worm burrows, swallows targets and attacks with spines. After emerging, it can tether nearby targets with Life Siphon. | Watch where the head and body surface, keep clear of the swallowing attack and spines, and retreat as soon as a siphon tether appears. |
| A target within 10 world units of any worm part can be siphoned. Each target takes 8 base damage every 0.5 seconds; the boss heals for twice the health actually lost. Multiple tethers on one target still deal damage only once per tick. | Move more than 10 world units away from **every** worm part to break the tether; moving away from the head alone is insufficient. Armour reduces the health drained. The 45-second cooldown starts only after every tether is gone. |
| Electricity on the head or body interrupts the siphon. Swallowing a lit explosive, Brightshade Bomb, Mandrake, Cooked Mandrake or Mushroom Bait makes the worm stagger while chewing. | Use electric attacks to break tethers, or exploit a stagger after it swallows one of these items. Stay clear of its swallowing range while doing so. |
| A single hit can remove at most 4% of its maximum health (currently 200). A hit above that cap also triggers pixel-damage retaliation based on the attempted damage. | Deal steady hits instead of relying on oversized bursts. |

## Rift Bat King: manage clones and debuffs

| Mechanic | Counter |
| --- | --- |
| At about two-thirds health it enters phase two. It can split over up to three rounds, producing as many as 7 clones; the boss and clones cannot be damaged during the split animation. | Wait until the split finishes before attacking. |
| Hitting the boss or a clone blocks the attacker's ordinary healing for about 30 seconds. Hitting a clone also poisons the attacker, and a bat hit on a player applies poison. | Heal before committing and focus on dodging during the healing lockout. Poison stacks up to 5 times; each stack lasts 60 seconds and deals 1 pixel damage every 3 seconds. Eating Taffy cures poison. |
| With clones present, hitting the **real boss** stuns it for 8 seconds; the stun then has a 10-second cooldown. | Identify the original and focus attacks on it to create a safe damage window. Blindly hitting clones adds poison. |
| In phase two the boss can attach to a player's head, then roar after detaching. The roar reaches about 24 world units. Fear lasts 20 seconds and slows movement and attacks while reducing damage dealt. | Retreat when the roar begins, or wear the base-game Earmuffs in advance to block fear. |
| Each hit can remove at most 4% of its maximum health (currently 480); larger hits trigger pixel-damage retaliation. | Avoid very large single-hit bursts. |

## Meteor Lobster King: crack the shell and mine the ores

| Mechanic | Counter |
| --- | --- |
| At around 16,000 health it enters phase two and repeatedly retreats into its shell at later damage thresholds. The shell makes it invulnerable and heals it for 5 health per second. | Hit the boss with an actual explosion to break the shell, then resume attacking. An explosive sitting nearby is insufficient unless its blast reaches the boss. Keep yourself and teammates clear of the blast. |
| From phase two, roughly every third melee attack is followed by an expanding semicircular quake in the direction it faces. The phase-three quake reaches farther, alongside charges, frontal attacks and knockback. | Move behind the boss or leave its forward arc when the quake starts; do not stand directly ahead of it. |
| Meteor rain begins after the third shell. At about 8,000 health, phase three also brings meteor rain and 3 guards. Impact points are marked in advance, and some impacts leave Resonant Meteor Boulders. | Keep moving when warnings appear, avoid impact points and guard charges, and assign someone to deal with the guards and boulders. |
| Each Resonant Meteor Boulder raises the boss's attack and effective defence by about 10%. | Mine the boulders with a pickaxe to remove the buffs. Its quake can also shatter boulders caught in the wave. |
| Ranged weapon hits reflect the incoming damage to the shooter. A single hit can remove at most 4% of maximum health (currently 860), and an oversized hit also triggers pixel-damage retaliation. | Use safe melee openings instead of trading ranged shots or trying to skip a phase with one huge hit. |

After all three bosses are defeated, the arena unlocks and a Pixel-Space Rift appears. Place 5 Celestial Jewels in the rift and accept the confirmation to start the Pixel Invasion. The offering consumes the jewels, so check your progress before confirming.

## Pixel corruption

When Charlie's expanding wave hits a player, pixel corruption begins to build. It rises one level per minute on the island and falls one level every 30 seconds after leaving. Reaching the maximum level is fatal. The Pixel Invasion is a separate stage started through the rift after the bosses; it does not replace leaving the island to clear a player's corruption. Teleportation restrictions apply during the active challenge.

> The island needs enough open ocean. If an existing world has no suitable placement space yet, the mod tries again when it loads next time.
