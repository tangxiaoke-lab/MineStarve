# The Deep Dark

Sail to the Sculk island, face three bosses in Charlie's arena, and decide whether to begin the Pixel Invasion.

## Finding the island and starting the challenge

The Deep Dark is a separate island at sea. It contains Sculk Ground, Sculk Veins and Charlie's arena. New worlds generate the island; eligible existing worlds also try to add it. Approaching Charlie starts the performance and battle sequence.

During the challenge, the arena restricts entry, exit and teleportation. If the party falls, it has a 60-second revival window; the challenge fails if nobody recovers in time. The next boss arrives about 10 seconds after the previous one is defeated.

## Boss order

| Order | Boss | Main mechanics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Void-Devouring Worm King | Burrowing, swallowing, spines and Life Siphon, which repeatedly drains nearby targets. |
| 2 | Rift Bat King | Summoned bats and clones, poison, healing lockout and fear. |
| 3 | Meteor Lobster King | Rock Lobster Guards, charges, quakes, meteor rain and Resonant Meteor Boulders. |

## Void-Devouring Worm King

| Mechanic | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Burrowing and swallowing | The worm burrows, swallows targets and attacks with spines. |
| Life Siphon | After emerging, it can tether targets within 10 world units of any body part. The tether deals 8 base damage every 0.5 seconds and heals the boss for twice the health actually lost. Multiple tethers on one target deal damage only once per tick. A tether breaks when the target is more than 10 world units from every worm part. Once all tethers are gone, the siphon enters a 45-second cooldown. |
| Interruption and stagger | Electricity on the head or body interrupts the siphon. Swallowing a lit explosive, Brightshade Bomb, Mandrake, Cooked Mandrake or Mushroom Bait makes the worm stagger while chewing. |
| Damage cap and retaliation | A single hit removes at most 4% of maximum health (currently 200). Hits above the cap also trigger pixel-damage retaliation based on the attempted damage. |

## Rift Bat King

| Mechanic | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Phase and clones | At about two-thirds health it enters phase two. It can split over up to three rounds and produce as many as 7 clones. The boss and clones cannot be damaged during the split. |
| Healing lockout and poison | Hitting the boss or a clone blocks the attacker's ordinary healing for about 30 seconds. Hitting a clone also poisons the attacker, and bat hits on players apply poison. Poison stacks up to 5 times; each stack lasts 60 seconds and adds 1 pixel damage to each three-second tick. |
| Stun on the real boss | With clones present, hitting the real boss stuns it for 8 seconds; this stun has a 10-second cooldown. |
| Attachment and roar | In phase two it can attach to a player's head, then roar after detaching. The roar reaches about 24 world units. Fear lasts 20 seconds, slows movement and attacks, reduces damage dealt, and drains sanity. |
| Damage cap and retaliation | A single hit removes at most 4% of maximum health (currently 480); larger hits trigger pixel-damage retaliation. |

## Meteor Lobster King

| Mechanic | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Phase and shell | At around 16,000 health it enters phase two and repeatedly retreats into its shell at later damage thresholds. While shelled, it is invulnerable and heals 5 health per second. An explosion event makes it leave the shell. |
| Charges and quakes | From phase two, roughly every third melee attack is followed by an expanding semicircular quake in the direction it faces. Phase-three quakes reach farther, and melee hits can launch targets. |
| Meteor rain and guards | Meteor rain begins after the third shell. At about 8,000 health it enters phase three and summons 3 guards. Each meteor wave contains about 12 meteors; impact points are marked in advance and some leave Resonant Meteor Boulders. |
| Resonant Meteor Boulders | Each boulder raises the boss's attack and effective defence by about 10%. Removing a boulder by mining or by the boss's quake removes its bonus. |
| Ranged reflection and damage cap | Ranged weapon hits reflect the incoming damage to the shooter. A single hit removes at most 4% of maximum health (currently 860); larger hits also trigger pixel-damage retaliation. Glass Cutter hits additionally consume half of its total durability. |

After all three bosses are defeated, the arena unlocks and a Pixel-Space Rift appears. Offering 5 Celestial Jewels to the rift and confirming consumes the jewels and starts the Pixel Invasion.

## Pixel corruption

When Charlie's expanding wave hits a player, pixel corruption begins to build. It rises one level per minute on the island and falls one level every 30 seconds after leaving. Reaching the maximum level is fatal. The Pixel Invasion is a separate stage started through the rift after the bosses; it does not replace leaving the island to clear a player's corruption. Teleportation restrictions apply during the active challenge.

> The island needs enough open ocean. If an existing world has no suitable placement space yet, the mod tries again when it loads next time.
