# Hunger and Sprinting

When Minecraft-style hunger is enabled, hunger also affects healing, sprinting, attacks and work.

## Healing outside combat

After at least 3 seconds outside combat, a player with at least 80% hunger can spend 2 hunger every 0.5 seconds to recover 1 health. Dropping below the threshold or returning to combat stops the recovery. Wanda does not heal this way and receives sprinting and consumption compensation instead.

## Sprinting and action costs

Hold the in-game sprint key to move 25% faster at a cost of 0.5 hunger per second. Sprinting stops below 20% of maximum hunger. With Wanda's compensation, the speed bonus is 30%.

| Action | Hunger cost |
| --- | --- |
| One successful attack | 0.33; a single area attack counts once |
| Effective digging, chopping, mining, harvesting or hammering | 0.2 per action |

Character abilities may add further hunger costs. Zombie hits apply a 10-second Hunger effect: 1 additional hunger is lost per second, and fullness-based healing is blocked. Further hits refresh its duration.

> These effects apply when the corresponding hunger gameplay is enabled in the world. Otherwise the base game's hunger rules apply.
